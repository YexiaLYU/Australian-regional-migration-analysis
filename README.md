# Australian Regional Migration Analysis

An R-based analysis of how overseas migration contributed to population growth across regional Australia in 2024–25, using Australian Bureau of Statistics population and spatial data.

## Project Overview

This project examines population growth patterns across regional Australian SA4 areas, with a particular focus on the contribution of net overseas migration.

The analysis addresses three questions:

1. How strongly is net overseas migration associated with regional population growth?
2. Which regional areas experienced the largest migration-driven population increases?
3. How does the contribution of overseas migration vary geographically?

## Key Findings

- The Gold Coast recorded the highest net overseas migration rate among regional SA4 areas, at 1.31%.
- Overseas migration made an important contribution to growth in several regional areas across Queensland, Western Australia and the Northern Territory.
- Regional population growth reflected different combinations of overseas migration, internal migration and natural increase.
- Overseas migration rates were positively associated with population growth, although the relationship varied considerably across regions.
